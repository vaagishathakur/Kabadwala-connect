# KabadConnect — Unit Economics Assessment

## Executive Summary

KabadConnect creates measurable economic value for informal collectors by improving price transparency, eliminating middleman markups, and connecting collectors directly with authorized recyclers at higher offered rates.

---

### Informal Chain Margins (What Collectors Lose)

```
Collector → Local Kabadiwala (−20%) → Aggregator (−15%) → Recycler
```

Collector receives only **~65% of the recycler-offered price** due to multi-level intermediaries.

## Field Research Summary

### Collector 1 — Ramesh, Mumbai (Dharavi area)
- **Years experience:** 8 years
- **Daily collection:** ~7 kg mixed e-waste
- **Current monthly income:** ₹7,500–9,000
- **Pain points:** "Kabadiwala gives me half the price. I don't know the real rate. Sometimes they say price is low but I don't know if it's true."
- **Smartphone:** Yes, basic Android (4G)
- **Literacy:** Can read simple Hindi; prefers pictures
- **Response to platform concept:** Very positive; "If I can see the price board daily, I will know when to sell and to whom."

### Collector 2 — Sunita, Pune (Hadapsar area)
- **Years experience:** 5 years
- **Collection focus:** Batteries, cables, PCBs
- **Current monthly income:** ₹6,000–7,500
- **Pain points:** "Recyclers don't want small quantities. I have to wait for local dealer. He takes his cut."
- **Smartphone:** Shared family phone, basic Android
- **Literacy:** Marathi comfortable; Hindi basic
- **Response to platform concept:** "If they come to pick up at my location, I will use it for sure. Going to recycler is too far."

### Key Insights from Field Research
1. **Price opacity** is the #1 complaint — collectors feel cheated without price knowledge
2. **Pickup availability** is critical — most cannot transport to recycler independently
3. **Trust** is built slowly — QR-based handover receipts would help prove fair dealing
4. **Cash remains preferred** — UPI is used but cash is expected for informal transactions
5. **Low-literacy UI** is essential — icon + audio must replace text wherever possible
6. **Vernacular first** — even bilingual collectors default to Hindi/Marathi for financial decisions
